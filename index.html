Mesonet Station Status

What this shows

Every Montana Mesonet station, colored by how recently it reported.

  • Status: reported in the last 2 hours, or not.
  • Time since: time since the last report.
  • Health: whether every expected sensor is reporting.
  • Power: the battery and charge controller at HydroMet stations.

A hollow dot has no record. In Power, it has no power data: AgriMet stations have none. A numbered dot is several stations at one site.

What the Health categories mean
  • Operational: reported in the last 2 hours with every sensor.
  • Partial — new sensor outage: a sensor is silent and not yet flagged.
  • Partial — known outage: every silent sensor is a flagged outage.
  • Total outage: no report in the last 2 hours.
What the Power categories mean
  • Power OK: none of the below.
  • Low battery: 24-hour minimum under 24.5 V (11.5 V at a voltage-only station).
  • Critical: 24-hour minimum under 23.5 V, or the controller has cut the load. It stays critical if the station then goes silent.
  • Power feed silent: no power record in 6 hours.

Power records arrive every 2 hours.

How to use it

  • Pick a mode in the bar.
  • Tap a station for its details and a dashboard link.
  • Tap a legend row to hide that category. Double-tap to show only it.
  • Tap HydroMet or AgriMet to show or hide that network.
  • Keys: / search, Esc close. Add ?kbd=off to the address to turn off /.
