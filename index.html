Mesonet Station Status

Live reporting status for the Montana Mesonet network

Modes

  • Status — green if reported in the last 2 hours, red otherwise.
  • Time since — 5 bins from <2 h to >24 h.
  • Health — Operational: reported in the last 2 hours with every expected sensor present. Partial: reported, but one or more sensors are silent (the popup lists them). Total outage: no report in the last 2 hours.

How to use it

  • Legend counts — every row shows how many of the selected networks' stations fall in that category, and the share.
  • Search — press / to jump there; ↑/↓ + Enter to pick.
  • Networks — chips toggle HydroMet / AgriMet.
  • Legend — click a row to hide a category; double-click (or Shift+Enter) to isolate.
  • Hover a dot for time since last report; click for full details + dashboard link.
  • Shapes — a filled dot is a HydroMet station, a dot with an orange ring an AgriMet station; the fill is always the status color.
  • Numbered dots — multiple stations share that location. Hover or tap to expand them; click one for its details.
  • Esc closes any popup or fan-out.
  • Single-key shortcuts can be disabled by adding ?kbd=off to the URL (it sticks while you browse).

Share or bookmark

The URL reflects every choice — copy it from the address bar. ?station=<id> deep-links a single station. Times are Mountain Time.
